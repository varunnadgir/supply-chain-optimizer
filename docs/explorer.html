Where should an online marketplace put its warehouses?

Olist, a Brazilian marketplace, ships every order straight from the seller, and 72% of items leave from São Paulo state. This page shows what an optimization model recommends instead: a few regional fulfillment centers that hold stock close to customers. Every scenario below was solved in advance from 84,523 real shipments (Sep 2017 to Aug 2018).

How the model works

  • Freight rate card. Parcel cost by distance zone and weight, fitted by least squares to the real shipments.
  • Two trips through a center. Seller to center in bulk by truck (cheap per item), then a short parcel trip to the customer, plus a handling cost.
  • Optimization. A mixed-integer program (the p-median problem with a "ship direct" option) chooses which sites to open and how to serve each of 3,811 cities. Solved with PuLP and CBC, and checked against brute force for small cases.

What to keep in mind

  • The handling cost is assumed, and it moves the answer the most. That is why it is a slider.
  • Rent and staffing for each center are not included. The saving each extra center adds is the most it could cost per year and still pay off.
  • Distances are straight-line, and centers have no capacity limit.
  • Holding stock in the centers costs roughly a quarter of the base case saving (notebook 3).
